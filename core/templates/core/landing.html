FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common Questions

Key details regarding StudySphere's synthesis engine and format support.

What file formats can I upload into StudySphere? +
StudySphere accepts PDF documents, Microsoft Word (.docx), plain text (.txt), Markdown (.md), CSV datasets, Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx), and raw pasted text. It automatically handles token extraction, paragraph structuring, and stopword filtering across all formats.
How does the Real-View Visualization engine work? +
Unlike static images, our Real-View engine renders dynamic client-side Chart.js canvases with interactive tooltips, percentage shares, multi-axis radar competency evaluations, and clickable nodes that instantly synchronize with the right-side Knowledge Lens inspector.
How fast is document processing? +
Processing is near instantaneous (under 3 seconds). Simply drop your file or paste your notes to immediately launch your workspace across all 9 synchronized modalities.
What is the Ebbinghaus Retention Matrix? +
It is a learning science feature based on Hermann Ebbinghaus's mathematical forgetting curve. It simulates how much information you retain over 1 to 30 days and calculates optimal spaced review sessions to prevent memory decay.
Can I export diagrams and printable cheatsheets? +
Yes. You can download high-resolution PNGs directly from the visual canvas, copy raw Mermaid diagram code, and generate clean, print-ready 1-page cheatsheets formatted for exam day.
